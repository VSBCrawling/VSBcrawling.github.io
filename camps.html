Our 3‑Hour Camp Experience

Give your campers the most unforgettable specialty program of the summer. VSB Entertainment delivers a fully‑run, fully‑supervised 3‑hour RC crawling experience that transforms any camp day into a high‑energy, skill‑building adventure.

We bring three complete obstacle course setups, each designed for different ages and skill levels:

Beginner Course — “I Can Do This!”

Smooth lanes, gentle hills, and wide turns help new drivers build confidence fast.

Intermediate Course — “Let’s Level Up”

Bridges, tunnels, uneven terrain, and tighter turns challenge campers to think, plan, and work together.

Advanced Course — “The Big Challenge”

Rock gardens, steep climbs, narrow bridges, and technical obstacles create a thrilling, team‑cheering experience.
