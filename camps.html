Our 3–4 Hour Camp Adventure 🏁

Starting at $600 for a 3–4 hour visit • groups of 100–120 campers ages 3–18, split into age groups

Give your campers the most unforgettable specialty program of the summer. VSB Entertainment delivers a fully‑run, fully‑supervised 3–4 hour remote control (RC) crawling experience that transforms any camp day into a high‑energy, skill‑building adventure.

It Kicks Off With a Show! 🏍️

Every camp visit starts with an exciting RC show: remote control monster trucks and motorcycles jumping ramps, to get campers fired up before they take the controls themselves.

We bring three complete obstacle course setups, each designed for different ages and skill levels:

Beginner Course — “I Can Do This!”

Smooth lanes, gentle hills, and wide turns help new drivers build confidence fast.

Intermediate Course — “Let’s Level Up”

Bridges, tunnels, uneven terrain, and tighter turns challenge campers to think, plan, and work together.

Advanced Course — “The Big Challenge”

Rock gardens, steep climbs, narrow bridges, and technical obstacles create a thrilling, team‑cheering experience.
