Camp RC Crawling Experience

VSB Entertainment offers a full 3-hour RC crawling program designed specifically for summer camps. We bring three complete obstacle course setups, each offering a different level of challenge and fun so every camper stays engaged.

Beginner Course — Confidence Builder

A smooth, flowing track with wide lanes, gentle hills, and easy turns. Perfect for younger campers or first-time drivers learning throttle control and steering.

Intermediate Course — Skills & Strategy

A more technical layout with tighter turns, small bridges, tunnels, and uneven terrain. Campers learn problem-solving, teamwork, and how to choose smart driving lines.

Advanced Course — The Ultimate Challenge

Rock features, steep climbs, narrow bridges, and technical obstacles. Campers love testing their skills and cheering each other on as they tackle the toughest course.
