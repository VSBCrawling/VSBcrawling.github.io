The Short Answer 🙌

RC stands for remote control (you’ll also hear “radio-controlled”). RC rock crawling is an off-road hobby where small remote control trucks climb over steep rocks, dirt trails and obstacle courses.

Kids drive them slowly and carefully up hills, across bridges and through tunnels. It’s all about skill and steady hands, not speed, so every kid can do it and nobody gets left behind.

Two of our trucks on one of our courses: a log bridge over the rocks.

Crawling, Not Racing 🐢

Most people picture RC cars zooming around a parking lot. Crawling is the opposite, and that’s what makes it great for kids.

🏎️ RC racing

  • Go as fast as you can
  • Quick reflexes win
  • Crashes end the fun fast

🚙 RC crawling

  • Go slow and steady
  • Plan your path, pick your line
  • Stuck? Back up and try again!

What Makes a Crawler Special? 🔧

A crawler isn’t a toy car. It’s a hobby-grade truck built to climb. Here’s the secret sauce, in plain words:

⚙️ Locked Axles All the wheels turn together, so the truck keeps its grip on slippery rocks.
🦘 Bendy Suspension The axles twist way up and down, so all four tires stay on the bumpy ground.
⚖️ Low & Heavy Weight sits low and up front, so the truck doesn’t tip over on steep climbs.
🪨 Real Terrain Rocks, dirt, ramps, bridges and tunnels make every run a little puzzle.

Quick Questions 🙋

Does my child need to have driven an RC truck before?
Nope! We teach everyone the basics, and the beginner course is made for brand-new drivers.
Do we need our own trucks?
No. We bring multiple high-quality RC crawlers for all skill levels, plus the courses.
Is it hard?
It’s easy to start and fun to get better at. Going slow is the whole idea, so younger kids do great.
How much space do you need?
A driveway, gym, field, basement, or open indoor space works great.
